What Ionelle does

Ionelle measures, explains and alerts you about the battery and the system of your Android phone, within what Android allows an app from Google Play to do. Estimated figures (for example, capacity or the health index) carry their margin of error and depend on what each manufacturer reports. What the app can’t do is in What Ionelle can’t do and in the app, in Settings > Help.

Ionelle Premium

  • Plans. Premium is a Google Play subscription with a monthly and an annual plan. In Spain it costs €0.99 a month or €7.99 a year; in other countries, the price Google Play shows. The price, period and renewal are shown on the plans screen and in Google Play before you confirm; the price that applies is the one Google Play shows.
  • Free trial. The annual plan includes 7 days free if your account has never had a subscription in Ionelle (one trial per app; Google Play decides eligibility). Before subscribing you will see the date and amount of the first charge. If you cancel before the trial ends, you pay nothing.
  • Automatic renewal. The subscription renews by itself at the end of each period, for the same amount, until you cancel it.
  • Reminders. Ionelle reminds you 2 days before the trial ends and 15 days before each annual renewal, with a notification and a notice in the app. The date we show is estimated: we work it out on the phone from the purchase date; the exact date is always in Google Play. These reminders only work for purchases made from the app on that phone (not after reinstalling or on another phone); Google Play also informs you about your subscriptions.
  • Cancelling. You can cancel at any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Ionelle > Cancel subscription. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it. After cancelling, you keep Premium until the end of the period already paid and you are not charged again.

  • Price changes. If the price of your subscription changes, we will tell you before it applies, and Google Play will inform you under its rules.
