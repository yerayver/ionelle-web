What Ionelle does and does not do

Ionelle measures, explains and alerts you about your Android phone's battery and system, within what Android allows an app to do. It does not speed up the phone, free RAM, cool the CPU or "repair" the battery. Estimated figures (for example capacity or the health index) come with their margin of error and depend on what each manufacturer reports. What the app cannot do is listed in Settings > Help > "What Ionelle can't do".
