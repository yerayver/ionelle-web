What Ionelle can’t do

Version 22 November 2026

Pending legal review. A legal adviser has not reviewed this text yet, and it may change before the public launch. If the Spanish and English versions differ, the Spanish one prevails.

Ionelle only promises what Android allows an app from Google Play to do. This is what it can’t do, and why. The same list is in the app, in Settings > Help > “What Ionelle cannot do”.

  1. Stop or limit charging. Android doesn’t allow apps on Google Play to do it; only root can, and Ionelle doesn’t use root. We warn you and take you to your phone’s own charge limit, if it has one.
  2. Free RAM, close other apps or speed up your phone. Since Android 14, an app can only stop its own processes, and force-stopping apps in advanced mode doesn’t make the phone faster.
  3. Cool down your phone. We only show you its thermal status.
  4. Measure the real energy each app uses without advanced mode. With it, the figure is an estimate based on the power profile your manufacturer provides.
  5. Read the official battery health, its manufacturing date or its first-use date without advanced mode. We give you an estimate with its margin.
  6. Guarantee that the estimated capacity is exact: the unit and the sign of the current reading vary by manufacturer.
  7. Open the Android/data and Android/obb folders without advanced mode.
  8. Clear the cache of other apps without advanced mode.
  9. See every installed app without advanced mode: only the ones with an icon on your home screen.
  10. Start when you plug in the charger unless you turned that on, or start a background service without an action of yours or an exemption from Android.
  11. Stop Android or your manufacturer from putting apps to sleep or closing them. We guide you and, in advanced mode, adjust it in a reversible way, with no guarantees.
  12. Guarantee today that advanced mode (Shizuku) works on Android 17.
  13. Tap through Settings for you: we don’t use the accessibility service.
  14. Scan all your photos and videos unless you choose the folders.
  15. Fix a system bug or roll back an update: we measure it and help you report it.
  16. Undo the changes of advanced mode if you uninstall Ionelle or delete its data: undo them first from the change log. In Settings you can only undo what Android allows (for example, enabling a disabled app); a preinstalled app uninstalled for your user can’t be restored from Settings: that needs ADB or, at worst, a factory reset.
  17. Search for files in the root of your storage or in Download (for example, downloaded APKs): Android’s folder picker doesn’t allow it, and we don’t use access to all files.

“Advanced mode” is the experimental mode that uses the third-party app Shizuku; it is optional and reversible. If anything on this list changes with a new Android version, we will update this page. Questions? soporte@ionelle.com.
