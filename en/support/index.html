Contact

Write to us at soporte@ionelle.com, in English or Spanish. We have no phone support. To help you sooner, tell us your phone model, Android version and Ionelle version (in Android Settings > Apps > Ionelle), and what happened.

Don’t send us data we don’t need. If you want to share the manufacturer report, check its content first.

Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel Premium?

In Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Ionelle > Cancel subscription. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period. More details in the terms.

I changed phones or reinstalled and I don’t see Premium

Use the same Google Play account and tap “Restore purchases” in Settings > Premium. You need a connection.

When does my subscription renew?

The exact date is in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Ionelle shows an estimated date and reminds you 2 days before the trial ends and 15 days before each annual renewal.

A payment failed or I want a refund

Google Play handles payments and refunds: check your payment method in Google Play or request the refund there. If that does not solve it, write to us.

How do I stop sending statistics or delete my data?

Turn statistics off in Settings > Privacy. Data on the phone is deleted when you uninstall or with Android Settings > Apps > Ionelle > Storage > Clear data. Everything else is in the privacy policy.

Why doesn’t Ionelle do X?

Often it is because Android doesn’t allow it for an app from Google Play. We explain it in What Ionelle can’t do.
