What stays on your phone

Ionelle measures and analyses your battery and your system on the device itself. We have no servers of our own and this data is not sent to anyone:

  • Battery, charging and discharging measurements and history.
  • If you grant usage access: only when the screen turns on and off, to work out idle drain. We do not store which apps you use.
  • The manufacturer report (PDF) is created on the phone. It only includes the list of your apps if you tick the box, and it only leaves the phone if you choose to share it.
  • Your settings. If Android backup is on, only the settings file is included in that backup (managed by Google in your account).

Usage statistics and crash reports (optional)

Only if you accept in the app (you can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy), Ionelle sends usage statistics (for example, which sections you open and, once subscriptions exist, in-app purchases and subscriptions: product and price) and crash reports to Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics), which process the data on our behalf. They carry a random identifier of this installation and basic device details, such as the model and the Android version; Google derives a country or approximate area from the IP address. Ionelle never adds the list of your apps or your notifications. Purpose: finding out what fails and which parts of the app are used, to improve it. Retention: Crashlytics, 90 days; statistics, the period we set in Google Analytics (not yet set). If you do not accept, nothing is sent.

Ads (free version only)

The free version will show ads from Google AdMob. Before loading them, where the law requires it, we ask for your consent with Google's tool (User Messaging Platform). To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK collects and shares with Google the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), your interactions with ads and the app, diagnostic data and the device advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. More information: how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Ionelle Premium does not load the ads SDK.

Today, in the test versions, neither ads nor statistics are active: the app sends no data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are paid through Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment account details.

How to delete your data

  • Data on the phone is deleted when you uninstall Ionelle, or with Android Settings > Apps > Ionelle > Storage > Clear data.
  • To stop sending statistics and crash reports, turn it off in Ionelle, in Settings > Privacy.
  • To exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, write to soporte@ionelle.com. Ionelle has no accounts: the data sent to Firebase only carries a random identifier of the installation and, until the app lets you include it in your request, we cannot find it. Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
  • The data collected by the ads SDK is processed by Google and does not reach us, so we cannot delete it; use Google's and Android's controls (for example, resetting or deleting the advertising ID, which does not delete what was already collected).

Children

Ionelle is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not designed for minors.

Changes

If what the app does with your data changes, we will update this page and its date before the change.
