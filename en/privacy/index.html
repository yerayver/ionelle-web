Privacy policy

Version 22 November 2026

Pending legal review. A legal adviser has not reviewed this text yet, and it may change before the public launch. If the Spanish and English versions differ, the Spanish one prevails.

In short

  • Ionelle measures your battery and your system on your phone. We have no servers of our own and no user accounts.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are only sent if you accept them, and you can withdraw that at any time.
  • The free version will show ads from Google AdMob, which collects data to show them. Ionelle Premium does not load the ads SDK.
  • Today, in the test versions, neither statistics nor ads are active.

What stays on your phone

This data is processed only on the device and is not sent to anyone, including us:

  • Battery, charging and discharging measurements and history.
  • If you grant usage access: only when the screen turns on and off, to work out idle drain. We do not store which apps you use.
  • The manufacturer report (PDF) is created on the phone. It only includes the list of your apps if you tick the box, and it only leaves the phone if you choose to share it.
  • If you choose folders to look for large or duplicate files, the analysis runs on the phone and only in those folders.
  • If you use advanced mode (Shizuku), the change log is kept on the phone.
  • Your subscription status (product, state, purchase date, plan, trial end and amount), so the app knows whether you have Premium while offline and can send renewal reminders. We do not store the purchase token, the order number or any account details.
  • Your settings and your choice about statistics. If Android backup is on, only the settings file is included in that backup (managed by Google in your account).

Ionelle does not read the content of your notifications, messages, photos, contacts or precise location, and does not sell data.

Usage statistics and crash reports (optional)

Only if you accept in the app (you can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy), Ionelle sends data to Google Firebase, which processes it on our behalf:

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

When the app crashes: the stack trace, the app state at that moment, device details (for example, model and Android version) and a random identifier of the installation. Kept for 90 days; after that Google starts deleting it from its systems.

Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics)

A random identifier of the installation, an approximate location (country or area) that Google derives from the masked IP address, screens and sessions, in-app purchases and subscriptions (product and price) and only two events of our own: that you finished the welcome flow and which sections you open. Analytics does not collect the advertising ID and we have turned Google Signals off (data is not linked to your Google account). User-level and event-level data is kept for 2 months and the period is not extended by new activity; aggregated reports, which identify no one, are not subject to that limit.

Ionelle never adds the list of your apps, your notifications or your battery measurements to this data. Purpose: finding out what fails and which parts of the app are used, to improve it. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time; withdrawing stops sending but does not delete what was already sent. If you do not accept, nothing is sent and the app works the same.

Ads (free version only)

The free version will show ads from Google AdMob; they are not active yet. They never appear in notifications, widgets or on the lock screen, and they are not loaded in the background. Before loading them, where the law requires it, we ask for your consent with Google's tool (User Messaging Platform), which stores your choice on the phone.

To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK collects and shares with Google the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), your interactions with the app and the ads (app launch, taps, video views), diagnostic data about the app and the SDK, the device advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers of the accounts on the device. Google also processes this data for its own purposes: how Google uses information from apps that use its services. If you refuse personalisation, ads are not personalised, but the SDK still collects data to show them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Ionelle Premium does not load the ads SDK: it is the way to use Ionelle without them.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought and paid through Google Play, which processes your payment data under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your card or payment account details. Ionelle asks Google Play whether you have an active subscription and keeps only what is listed above, on the phone. Legal basis: the subscription contract. The conditions are in the terms of use.

Who receives data and where

  • Google (Firebase), as processor, only if you accept statistics.
  • Google (AdMob), in the free version, for its advertising, analytics and fraud prevention purposes.
  • Google Play, for purchases.

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. Google states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

How to delete your data and your rights

  • Data on the phone is deleted when you uninstall Ionelle or with Android Settings > Apps > Ionelle > Storage > Clear data. If you use advanced mode, undo its changes first from the change log: clearing the data does not revert them (see what Ionelle can’t do, item 16).
  • To stop sending statistics and crash reports, turn it off in Ionelle, in Settings > Privacy.
  • You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent, by writing to soporte@ionelle.com. Ionelle has no accounts: data sent to Firebase only carries a random identifier of the installation and, until the app lets you include it in your request, we cannot locate it. Crash reports delete themselves after 90 days and user-level statistics after 2 months.
  • Data collected by the ads SDK is processed by Google and does not pass through us, so we cannot delete it; use Google's and Android's controls (resetting or deleting the advertising ID stops new data being linked to that ID, but does not delete what was already collected).
  • You can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or the authority of your country.

Minors

Ionelle is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not meant for minors.

Changes

If what the app does with your data changes, we will update this page and its date before the change and, if your consent is needed, ask for it again in the app.
